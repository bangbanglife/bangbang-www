FAQ

Still curious?

How do I post a task that needs several people?

A Task has one chosen Service Provider, and only they are paid; BangBang does not split payments. Say in the description how many people you need: the Service Provider can bring helpers and quote for the whole job. Helpers need your agreement first, and the chosen Service Provider stays responsible. If you need two independent Service Providers, post two Tasks.

What's the difference between Fixed price and Open offers?

You choose one when you post. Fixed price: you set the price, Service Providers apply at that price, and you pick one of them. Open offers: leave the price blank, Service Providers each make an offer, and you choose after checking their prices, reviews and messages.

What's the difference between online and offline tasks?

Online tasks don't need a meet-up, so Service Providers anywhere in Australia can take them. Offline tasks are done in person, by a Service Provider nearby.

Do I have to set a time?

No. If you set one, the Service Provider works to it. If you don't, it's done as soon as possible.

Can I post tasks and take jobs too?

Yes. One account can post tasks and take on other people's. Before you take jobs, set up payouts in the app. You can't take a task you posted yourself.
