4. Posting tasks, content checks and user content

4.1 Tasks must be lawful and comply with the Platform's content rules. Content prohibited by law cannot be posted. Tasks the Platform does not accept include: work that needs a licence (for example electrical, plumbing or gas work); regulated items (prescription medicines, tobacco, alcohol, weapons, gambling, lottery tickets); legal, visa and migration, medical, financial or tax advice; academic dishonesty such as ghost-writing or exam-taking; and bulk business labour or commercial rostering. The prompts in the app set out the detail. The Platform runs automated content checks and risk grading on posted content.

4.2 A task that passes the automated check is published immediately. A task that is clearly prohibited, or that the Platform does not accept, is not published; a task that the system cannot confirm may be published is also not published and not opened for offers. In either case the system prompts the Task Poster to edit and resubmit, and the resubmitted task goes through the automated check again. If you believe the automated result is wrong, you may give feedback to customer support, which will reply; whether the task is published depends on the automated check of the resubmitted task.

4.3 Zero tolerance for user content: the Platform has zero tolerance for prohibited content and abusive conduct. Any user may report a task, review or chat content that breaks the rules, and may block other users, at the places shown in the app. A person you have blocked cannot message you or apply for your tasks. You cannot block someone while a task with them is in progress; if you have a problem during a task, report it or contact customer support. The Platform handles reports promptly. It may take content down, and may restrict or ban accounts under clause 3.5. All such actions are recorded.

4.4 You are responsible for the content you post, and you warrant that it is lawful, accurate and does not infringe anyone else's rights.

4.5 Carrying passengers for payment: a task in which the Service Provider drives a motor vehicle carrying the Task Poster or anyone else cannot be posted or taken on through the Platform, whoever owns the vehicle. This includes driving people for a fee, acting as a designated driver, and driving someone to or from medical appointments. The Platform's automated content check blocks such tasks (see 4.2), and the Platform may take a task down if it finds one or receives a report (see 4.3); if such a task has already been paid for, the Platform may cancel it and return the full amount to the Task Poster as those Rules provide (see clause 7.9 of the Refund and Dispute Rules). Tasks to carry goods or items are still allowed, but the Task Poster and other people must not ride in the Service Provider's vehicle; anyone who needs to go along should make their own way there.

Where both parties agree to reschedule under clause 5.4 of the Refund and Dispute Rules, the contract itself is not altered; the Platform keeps a separate rescheduling record, and the task time is as shown in that record.

The Platform does not keep users' task funds in its own bank account. Funds are released when the Task Poster confirms completion, when 48 hours have passed after submission and the conditions for automatic confirmation are met, or when the Platform decides on release. Funds that have been released and are available for payout are scheduled daily for payout to the Service Provider's bank account. Actual receipt depends on the payment provider and the receiving bank. A full refund after the charge goes to the original payment method. Once funds are released, the ordinary refund process within the Platform ends. Statutory remedies, correction of technical or operational errors, and chargeback handling under clause 6.5 are unaffected. See sections 3 and 4 of the Refund and Dispute Rules.

On release, the Service Provider receives the task amount less the platform service fee; when making an offer or taking on a task, the Service Provider is shown the amount they will receive.

(a) Rate: the platform service fee is a percentage of the task amount, with a maximum per task. The rate and maximum that apply are those published by the Platform on its fees page (when this version was published: 12% of the task amount, up to A$45 per task). Each task is charged at the rate in force when the Service Provider submitted the offer or application for that task; if the Service Provider later changed the offer amount, at the rate in force when the amount was last changed. The Platform records the rate that applies to each offer.

(b) Platform service fee discounts: the Platform may offer Service Providers a platform service fee discount. There are currently two: the "Regulars benefit" and the "BB Voucher". On a task to which a discount applies, the platform service fee is 2% of the task amount plus A$0.30. A task on which that amount would be higher than the standard platform service fee does not get the discount and does not use up a BB Voucher. Whether a discount applies to a task is decided when the task is paid for; which tasks it applies to, and how a discount is obtained and used, are set out in clauses 6.8 and 6.9.

(c) Payment processing costs included: the platform service fee includes all payment processing costs. Where payment processing costs are higher, for example because the Task Poster uses an international card, the Platform bears the difference and the amount the Service Provider will receive is not affected. The Platform charges the Service Provider no other fee.

(d) What the Task Poster pays: the Task Poster pays the task amount agreed in the task contract, with nothing added on top. The task amount is a GST-inclusive total; where the Service Provider is required by law to charge GST, it is included.

(e) Changes to the rate: the Platform may change the platform service fee rate and maximum. An increase in the rate or the maximum is published on the fees page, and notified to Service Providers by in-app notice, at least 30 days before it takes effect; a decrease may take effect when it is published. A Service Provider who does not accept an increase may stop making new offers before it takes effect, or delete their account under clause 3.4. A change does not affect tasks already formed, or offers submitted before the change takes effect.

(f) The platform service fee includes GST where applicable. Once the Platform is registered for GST, it will give the Service Provider platform service fee documents that can be used as tax invoices.

7. Location and privacy

7.1 To protect users, the exact location of a task is revealed in stages. Only after payment and confirmation of the task is the necessary exact location shown to the matched Service Provider.

7.2 Full addresses are stored encrypted, and every access to an exact address is logged. After a task is completed, the Service Provider can view the exact address only for 7 days after completion, and it is not shown to the Service Provider after that; the Task Poster can continue to see the address they entered. See the Privacy Policy for details.
